Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who uses PostaAi

PostaAi is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

YouTube API Services

PostaAi uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels. By using PostaAi you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information accessed, collected and stored

  • OAuth tokens granted by the channel owner when connecting a channel (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • Channel ID and channel title of the connected channel, read once to show which channel was connected.
  • IDs of the videos it uploads, to link to them.

PostaAi does not access any other data from the YouTube account, and no data from other YouTube users.

How the information is used

Only to upload, to the owner's own channels, the videos the owner produced, with the title, description, privacy setting and publishing time the owner chose, and to show the owner the upload result.

Sharing

The information is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party. It is sent only to Google, as part of the YouTube API requests.

Storage, security and deletion

All information is stored only in a local database on the owner's computer, which is not reachable from the internet. Disconnecting a channel in PostaAi deletes its stored tokens and channel data. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), after which PostaAi can no longer use it.

Cookies and tracking

PostaAi does not use cookies, analytics or any kind of tracking. It does not store or read information on other people's devices.

Limited use

PostaAi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Resumo em português

O PostaAi é uma ferramenta pessoal, de um único usuário. Ele guarda, só no computador do dono, os tokens do Google e o nome e o ID dos canais conectados, para enviar ao YouTube os vídeos que o próprio dono produz. Nada é compartilhado com terceiros, não há cookies nem rastreamento, e o acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento em myaccount.google.com/permissions. O uso dos serviços de API do YouTube segue os Termos de Serviço do YouTube e a Política de Privacidade do Google.
